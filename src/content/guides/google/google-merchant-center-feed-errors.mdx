## How Merchant Center reads your file

A product file for Merchant Center has one product per line and Google's attribute names in the first row: `id`, `title`, `price` and so on. Google accepts a tab-delimited text file (.txt), and the attribute names are always in English, whatever language your products are in.

After an upload, Google checks every product and lists the problems under **Products → Needs attention**. A problem on one product doesn't stop the others, so a file can be "accepted" while half of it is disapproved. Most disapprovals come from a short list of formatting mistakes, covered below.

These attributes are needed for every product:

| Attribute | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| id | MUG-01 | Up to 50 characters, unique, and stable over time |
| title | Acme Ceramic Coffee Mug, 12 oz | Up to 150 characters |
| description | Hand-thrown stoneware mug… | Up to 5000 characters |
| link | https://shop.example.com/products/mug | The product page |
| image_link | https://shop.example.com/images/mug.jpg | The main image |
| availability | in_stock | One of Google's four values |
| price | 18.00 USD | A number and a currency code |

New products also need `brand`, and products with a barcode need `gtin`.

## Price without a currency code

**Cause:** The price is a bare number (`18.00`), or uses a `$` sign. Several countries use dollars, so Google can't tell which one you mean.

**Fix:** Write the number with a period for decimals, a space, and the ISO 4217 code: `18.00 USD`, `24.95 CAD`, `19.99 EUR`. Don't use thousand separators: `1299.00 GBP`, not `1,299.00 GBP`.

For discounts, keep the regular price in `price` and put the lower price in `sale_price`. If `sale_price` isn't lower than `price`, Google ignores it.

---

## Availability and preorder dates

**Cause:** `availability` holds a store's own wording, such as `In Stock`, `sold out` or `Available`. Google only accepts four values: `in_stock`, `out_of_stock`, `preorder` and `backorder`.

**Fix:** Replace the values with Google's. For `preorder`, also add `availability_date`, the date the product will ship, in ISO 8601 format: `2026-12-01T09:00-0500`. Google asks for the date on `backorder` too.

`condition` works the same way: only `new`, `used` and `refurbished` are accepted.

---

## GTINs and identifiers

**Cause:** The `gtin` has the wrong number of digits or a wrong check digit. Spreadsheets cause most of these: a 13-digit barcode is shown as `4.00638E+12` and saved that way, and UPCs lose their leading zero.

**Fix:**
- Format barcode columns as Text before pasting, so the digits are kept
- Copy barcodes again from the packaging or your supplier if they already show in scientific notation
- Remove spaces and dashes: `4006381333931`, not `4006-3813-3393-1`

A product with no barcode should have its manufacturer part number in `mpn`. For handmade, custom or vintage items with neither, set `identifier_exists` to `no`. Don't make up a GTIN: Google checks them against the GS1 database.

---

## Images and links

**Cause:** `link` or `image_link` is a relative path such as `mug.jpg`, or the image is behind a login or blocked from crawlers.

**Fix:** Use the full address, starting with `https://`, for the product page and every image. Up to 10 extra images can go in `additional_image_link`, separated by commas. Open a few links in a private browser window to check they load.

## Variants: item_group_id

A shirt in three sizes is three products for Google, each with its own `id`, `price` and `availability`. To show them as one product with options, give all three the same `item_group_id`, and fill in the attribute that differs, such as `size` or `color`.

### Example

```
id         item_group_id  title                     size  price
TEE-S      TEE            Organic Cotton Tee - S    S     24.00 USD
TEE-M      TEE            Organic Cotton Tee - M    M     24.00 USD
TEE-L      TEE            Organic Cotton Tee - L    L     24.00 USD
```

For clothing, Google also asks for `color`, `size`, `gender` and `age_group` in many countries. `gender` takes `male`, `female` or `unisex`, and `age_group` takes `newborn`, `infant`, `toddler`, `kids` or `adult`.

## Before you upload

1. Check that every price has a currency code
2. Check that `availability` and `condition` only use Google's values
3. Check that every barcode has 8, 12, 13 or 14 digits and none show as `E+12`
4. Check that links and images start with `https://`
5. Save as tab-delimited text (.txt)
